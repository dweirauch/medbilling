Why this matters

A single itemized bill can have dozens of lines, each with its own procedure code, a quantity, and a price — and no explanation of what any of it means. Errors hide in that noise: a lab test billed twice, a code that doesn't match what was actually done, a charge that should've been bundled into another. Individually, most of these look like a rounding error. Added up across a whole bill, they routinely run into hundreds of dollars — and the only way to catch them is to read every line the way a billing specialist would.
